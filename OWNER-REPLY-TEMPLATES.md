# WhatsApp Business Quick Replies

Create these under **WhatsApp Business → Settings → Business tools → Quick replies**.
Replace the bracketed values before sending. The cafe must verify the payment
in its bank app before confirming receipt.

keep the order ID as the reference. We will confirm after checking our bank app.
